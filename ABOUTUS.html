Who we are

WattWise was created to solve a problem that almost every Australian household knows too well - unexpectedly high energy bills with no clear idea why. After surveying real users and building empathy-based personas, our team discovered that most people are not wasteful; they are simply lost in confusing terminology, hidden peak-time rates, and bills that arrive with no context.

We started this project in our Design Thinking in IT course at Griffith University, where we followed a human-centred design process: empathise, define, ideate, prototype, and test. Every feature on this site - from the live price-comparison tool to the appliance energy tracker - was built around a real frustration we heard from real households.

What makes WattWise different is that we don't drown you in dashboards. Our design principle is calm clarity: plain language, simple visuals, and one helpful nudge at a time. Whether you're a renter in an older Queenslander, a parent juggling appliance loads, or a student watching every dollar, WattWise is built for you.

Our Mission & Vision

🎯

Mission

To make managing household energy use simple, transparent, and stress-free. We turn confusing bills and complex tariffs into clear everyday actions that help families save money and reduce waste - without sacrificing comfort.

🌱

Vision

A future where every household - including renters and lower-income families - has the same clear understanding of their energy use as their landlord or provider. We see WattWise growing into a trusted national tool for smarter, fairer energy decisions.

What we stand for

Our values come straight from the users we spoke to during our design thinking research. Real Australians told us they wanted information they could actually understand, tools that respected their time, and advice that worked for renters as well as homeowners.

Every design decision on WattWise - from our calm colour palette to our plain-English explanations - was made with these values in mind.

Energy Insights

Simple and practical tips to help households reduce electricity usage without feeling overwhelmed.

Why This Matters

These tips help users make small changes that can reduce energy costs. The information is kept short, clear and visual so users can understand it quickly.

Smart Energy Tips

⚡

Use Off-Peak Hours

Run appliances like washing machines and dishwashers during cheaper off-peak times.

🔌

Turn Off Standby

Switch devices off at the wall when not in use to avoid wasting power.

🌱

Use Eco Mode

Use energy-saving settings on appliances to reduce electricity use easily.

🔥

High Usage Devices

Air conditioners, heaters, dryers and ovens often use the most energy.
